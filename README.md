# Task 13
## Superstore Executive Dashboard

### 1. Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales, profitability bottlenecks, regional performance, and discount impacts using Tableau Public. The objective is to uncover hidden business insights and explore relationships between product sub-categories, regional sales distribution, and discounting strategies without relying on predefined questions.

### 2. Tools Used
* Tableau Public
* Microsoft Excel / Superstore Dataset
* Exploratory data analysis and interactive data visualization

### 3. Visualizations Created
* **Profit by Sub-Category:** A bar chart tracking profitable versus loss-making product lines to highlight margin leakage.
* **Sales & Profit by Region & Category:** A multi-bar comparison examining revenue and earnings across different geographical sectors and product types.
* **Discount vs. Profit:** A scatter plot investigating the direct negative correlation between heavy discounting and net product profit.
* **Regional Performance Map:** A geographic map mapping out sales volume (by dot size) and profit distribution (by color coding) across U.S. states.

### 4. Interactive Filters
* Region
* Category

These filters allow users to dynamically slice and explore different segments of the retail data.

### 5. Key Findings
* **Product Profitability Discrepancy:** High sales volume does not guarantee profitability. Sub-categories like Tables and Bookcases generate substantial revenue but suffer from negative profit margins due to aggressive discounting.
* **Discount Impact:** Heavy discounting heavily correlates with profit loss, dragging down otherwise high-performing product groups.
* **Geographic Variations:** Certain high-volume states (such as Texas and Illinois) show significant margin pressures compared to top-performing blue regions.

### 6. Suggestions and Recommendations
* Management should immediately re-evaluate pricing and discounting strategies for furniture sub-categories to curb margin leakage.
* Reallocate marketing budgets and inventory focus exclusively toward high-margin technology products (such as copiers and phones).
* Implement automated alert thresholds for regions experiencing high sales volume paired with low or negative profitability.

### 7. Conclusion
This analysis explores the major operational and financial factors affecting the Superstore dataset. The interactive dashboard helps stakeholders evaluate product performance, regional distribution, and discount efficiency to support more informed strategic business decisions.
